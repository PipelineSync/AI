2What Nova does

Nova is an AI assistant. It runs a short discovery call with you, by voice or by typing, about how your business finds, manages and closes deals. From your answers it prepares a blueprint that can include:

  • a recommended HubSpot setup and pipeline design,
  • automation opportunities and priority actions,
  • estimates of business impact and the cost of inaction, and
  • an option to book a consultation with the PipelineSync team.

The session is free. Booking a consultation does not commit you to buy anything.

4Your information

To run the session and follow up with you, we handle your information as follows:

  • CRM. Your name, email and answers are saved to our CRM (HubSpot) so our team can follow up with you.

  • Blueprint. Your blueprint is generated by Anthropic's Claude from your answers.
  • Audio. Audio is never stored. Only the text transcript of your answers is kept.

Before the call starts you will see a consent screen that repeats these points and asks for your agreement. Do not share passwords, payment card details, health information or other sensitive personal data during the call. Where a Privacy Policy is linked on the sign-in screen, it explains in more detail how we handle personal data and how to ask for access to or deletion of it.

Check anything important before acting on it. You are responsible for the decisions you make using the blueprint.
